# Bat Siege — art guide

Target look: `reference/concept.jpg`. Stylized low-poly 3D, chunky readable shapes, moonlit night,
warm glowing accents (torches, moths, bat eyes) against cool blue-green darkness. Spooky-cute,
never scary. Every gameplay object must read clearly on a phone at a glance.

## What to keep from the concept art, and what to change

**Keep:** the high bird's-eye camera, dark pine forest border, winding dirt path, glowing pond with
the moon's reflection, chunky brick castles with crenellations and arched torch-lit doorways, purple
ghost, glowing orange moths, the glass HUD and tray.

**Change (for gameplay readability):**
- The concept shows every block as grey stone. In the game, **materials must be instantly
  distinguishable**: wood = orange planks + red-brown gable roof, stone = grey bricks + crenellations,
  bone = cream bone bricks + skull + horned dome.
- Each grid square holds one clear block. The 6x3 grid must be readable, not a merged castle.
- Reinforced (level 2) blocks are visibly twice as tall.

## Lighting and render settings (Three.js)

- Renderer: sRGB output, ACES filmic tone mapping, exposure ~1.2, antialias on.
- Moonlight: directional `#c4d2ff`, intensity ~1.25, from up-right-back (8, 18, -8), soft shadows
  (PCFSoft), shadow camera covering the field (±15).
- Hemisphere light: sky `#6a82c8`, ground `#0c1a10`, intensity ~0.6.
- Faint warm fill from front-left `#ff9b5a` at ~0.18.
- Fog `#0a1424` from 34 to 60 units.
- Glows are additive sprites (radial gradient texture), not real lights: torches, moths, eyes,
  ghost, pond, moon reflection, orbs.
- Optional polish once the loop is fun: subtle bloom post-process on emissives (test phone fps first).

## Camera

- Perspective, FOV ~38°, looking down at ~65° from the player's side
  (direction vector (0, 24, 10.5) normalized), target slightly toward the player's half.
- Fit both castles on screen: distance = max(12.8 / tan(fov/2), 7.2 / (tan(fov/2) x aspect)).
  This keeps phones in portrait and desktops in landscape both framed.
- Small positional shake on impacts.

## Visual effects

- **Debris:** small cubes in the block's color, launched up and out, gravity, bounce, shrink out.
- **Sparkles:** additive sprites that drift and fade (perfect hits, moth pickups, bone healing).
- **Orb trail:** purple sparkles every 0.05 s behind ghost orbs.
- **Landing ring:** flat ring + center dot on the ground, pulsing, colored by target state.
- **Damage tint:** blocks darken as HP drops (lerp color toward 42% brightness).
- **Termites:** tiny red bugs circling the block's sides at different heights + a red glow.

## UI style

- Fonts (Google Fonts): **Lilita One** for display (title, labels, numbers), **Fredoka** for body.
- Glass panels: rgba(14,22,40,0.72) with a faint light border and blur; rounded 15–20 px.
- Tray buttons 62 px square with an isometric icon and a cost badge; attack buttons tinted red.
- Teal for the player, ember orange for the rival, moth amber for currency, ghost lavender.
- Text has a 2 px dark drop shadow so it reads over the 3D scene.
- Icons for the tray are SVGs in `reference/prototype.html` (reuse them).

## Optional higher-fidelity path

If time allows after the loop is fun: bake the procedural detail into textures (normal + color maps)
on simplified meshes for better phone performance, or hand-tweak the Blender output. Do not start
this before milestone 3.
